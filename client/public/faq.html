Family Fun FAQ

Is Family Fun free?

Yes. Both Tunk and Pitty Pat are free to play in your browser, 1v1 or 4-player, with no download. You can start playing as a guest without an account. Optional one-time purchases in the Store unlock card styles and table themes or remove ads, but buying anything is never required to play or to win.

Are the points real money?

No. Points are the score and nothing else. Number cards are worth 5 points, 10s and face cards 10, and Aces 15. Everybody starts a match with 5 points; first to 150 wins a 1v1 match and first to 300 wins a 4-player match. Points cannot be bought, sold, transferred, or cashed out, and there are no cash prizes. If anyone offers to sell you points or buy yours, they are not part of this game.

What is the difference between Tunk and Pitty Pat?

Tunk is about spreads and timing: build three or four of a kind or runs in the same suit, hit other players' spreads, and drop when you believe your unmatched total is lowest. Pitty Pat is about pairs: draw and discard until your hand holds three pairs, then call Pitty Pat. The How to Play page gives the full rules and strategy for both.

How do Family Rooms work?

Tap Family Rooms, create a room, and share the 6-letter code with your family. They open Family Fun, tap Family Rooms, and type the code to join your table. Rooms include table chat checked by a word filter and optional voice chat. Voice is live between devices and is not recorded. You can mute your own mic, silence a player for yourself, or report anyone who breaks the Keep It Cool rules.

Do I need an account?

No — guests can play right away, and guest points are kept on the device. An account saves your points, levels, and purchases across devices and is required to buy anything in the Store. You can delete your account at any time in the game under My Account, which permanently removes your progress and purchases.

What can I buy in the Store?

One-time purchases only — there is no subscription. Remove Ads is $0.99. The Royal Felt & Deck Pack is $1.49. The Family Fun Pass is $1.99 and unlocks the standard card styles and table themes and removes ads. The Family Host Pack is $4.99, and the C.F.N.B. Founder's Lifetime Bundle is $9.99. Purchases are tied to your signed-in account and can be restored on any device. Nothing in the Store buys points or changes who wins a hand.

Is Family Fun safe for kids?

Accounts for players under 13 cannot buy anything, chat with quick-talk phrases only, and cannot join voice chat. Table chat for everyone is checked by a word filter before it posts, and reports add up to strikes and a 24-hour cool-down from chat and voice. A parent or guardian can ask for an account to be deleted by emailing alquincy80@gmail.com.

Why does the game sometimes take a moment to load?

Family Fun runs on a free hosting plan that rests when nobody is playing. The first visit after a quiet spell can take up to about a minute to wake the game up. Once it loads, hands play at normal speed.
