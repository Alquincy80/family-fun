About Family Fun

Family Fun — Pitty Pat or Tunk — Your Move is a free card game that runs in your browser, made by Albert Q. Lightfoot. It brings two card games from his family's table to your phone or computer: Tunk, a game of spreads, drops, and nerve, and Pitty Pat, a quick pairing game where three pairs takes the hand. You can play either game 1v1 against Dee or in a 4-player game against Marcus, Dee, and Trey, or gather your own family at a private table in a Family Room.

Points, not money

Family Fun is played for points and only for points. Every match starts each player with 5 points. Number cards are worth 5 points, 10s and face cards are worth 10, and Aces are worth 15. The first player to 150 points wins a 1v1 match; the first to 300 wins a 4-player match. Points are earned by playing. They cannot be bought with real money, they cannot be sold, and they can never be cashed out. There is no gambling in Family Fun and there are no cash prizes — the points are the score, and the score is the fun.

Two games, one table

Tunk rewards patience and timing. You build spreads — three or four of a kind, or runs in the same suit — hit other players' spreads to shed heavy cards, and decide when to drop and claim the lowest unmatched total. Drop right and you take the hand; drop wrong and you pay double. Pitty Pat is faster: hold three pairs and call it. Every turn you draw and discard, trading loose cards for pairs while watching what everyone else throws away. The full rules for both games are on the How to Play page.

Family Rooms, chat, and voice

Family Rooms are private tables for your own people. Create a room and share its 6-letter code; family members open Family Fun, tap Family Rooms, and type the code to join. Rooms include table chat that is checked by a word filter before it posts, plus optional voice chat so the table can talk live. Voice goes directly between devices and is not recorded. The Keep It Cool rules apply everywhere: no cussing, insults, or bullying, with mute, silence, block, and report controls in every player's hands. Players under 13 chat with quick-talk phrases only, cannot join voice, and cannot buy anything.

Levels and rewards

Winning points also moves you up the levels. Each level unlocks a card style or a table theme, earned free by playing. Optional one-time purchases in the Store — such as the Family Fun Pass and Remove Ads — unlock standard styles and remove ads; they never buy points and never change who wins a hand. The game is free to play without buying anything, and no account is needed to start a game as a guest.
